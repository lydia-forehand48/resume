Tom Yum Goong: A Spicy Thai Delight

Tom Yum Goong is a famous Thai soup that incorporates shellfish to give it its signature hot and sour flavor.

The key to a delicious Tom Yum Goong lies in the balance of flavors – sweet, sour, salty, and spicy.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Shrimp stock 4 cups
Shrimp 15 pieces
Lime leaves 6 leaves
Galangal 8 slices
Thai chilies 5 chilies
Mushrooms 7 oz
Chili paste 1/4 cup
Fish sauce 3 tablespoons
Lime juice 1/2 cup
Sugar 1 teaspoon
Cilantro Until satisfied

Prepration

  1. Peel and devein shrimp before adding the shells and heads to a stock pot.
  2. Saute the shells in oil until orange.
  3. Add water and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Prepare the herbs and vegetables.
  5. Scoop out shells from stock pot.
  6. Add 4 cups of stock, herbs, chilies, and mushrooms to a pot, then simmer for 5 minutes.
  7. Add fish sauce and Thai chili paste.
  8. Bring soup to a boil, then add shrimp.
  9. Cook shrimp until halfway done, then turn off the heat.
  10. Allow the shrimp to sit in the soup until fully cooked.
  11. Add seasonings until satisfied.
  12. Stir in cilantro.
  13. Enjoy!
